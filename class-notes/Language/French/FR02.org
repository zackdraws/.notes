****** French Phrases
******* Bonjour ! Je voudrais réserver une grande chambre, s'il vous plaît.**
   - This phrase is a polite way to request a hotel room reservation in French.
     "Bonjour" means "hello."
     "Je voudrais" means "I would like."
     "Réserver" means "to book" or "to reserve."
     "Une grande chambre" means "a large room."
     "S'il vous plaît" is a polite way of saying "please."
******* Combien de nuits est-ce que vous voulez rester ?**
   - In this sentence, you're inquiring about the number of nights the person wishes to stay.
     "Combien de nuits" means "how many nights."
     "Est-ce que" is a question form.
     "Vous voulez" means "you want." "Rester" means "to stay."
******* Une chambre pour trois nuits, s'il vous plaît.**
   - This is a response to the previous question.
     It means "A room for three nights, please."
     "Une chambre" is "a room." "Pour" means "for."
     "Trois nuits" is "three nights."
******* Quel est le mot de passe du Wi-Fi ?**
   - Here, you're asking for the Wi-Fi password.
     "Quel" means "what." "Est" is a form of "être," which means "is."
      "Le mot de passe" is "the password."
      "Du Wi-Fi" indicates the Wi-Fi.
******* Le Wi-Fi ne marche pas.**
    "Ne marche pas" means "doesn't work."
******* Est-ce que le petit déjeuner est compris ?**
   - Here, you're inquiring whether breakfast is included.
     "Est-ce que" is a question form.
     "Le petit déjeuner" is "breakfast."
     "Est compris" means "is included."
******* Adjectives and their placement:**
   - This section explains that in French, adjectives typically come after the noun.
     However, certain adjectives that convey beauty, age, goodness, or size come before the noun.
     For example, "beaux gâteaux" means "beautiful cakes," and "un petit hôtel" means "a small hotel."
******* Articles:**
   - This section discusses the use of articles in French. It highlights that "le" is used with preferences,
      and "de" is used when implying "some" amount of a thing.
      For instance, "Ils préfèrent le chocolat" means "They prefer chocolate,"
       while "J'achète du chocolat" means "I'm buying (some) chocolate."
