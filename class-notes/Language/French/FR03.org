** Week 1-2: Adjective Agreement & Basic Vocabulary

*** Lesson 1: Adjective Agreement Basics

    - Focus on adjective endings (gender and number).


    - Learn basic adjectives and how to match them with nouns:

      - **gentil (m)** – kind

      - **gentille (f)** – kind

      - **beau (m)** – beautiful

      - **belle (f)** – beautiful

      - **grand (m)** – tall, big

      - **grande (f)** – tall, big

    - Practice with sentences:

      - **Il est gentil.** (He is kind.)

      - **Elle est gentille.** (She is kind.)

    - **Checkpoint**: Write 5 sentences using the adjectives above.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-01-30 08:00>

** Week 3-4: Comparative & Superlative Forms + Passé Composé

*** DONE Lesson 5: Comparative Forms (Better, More, Less)
    - Learn how to form comparisons using *plus* (more), *moins* (less), and *meilleur* (better):
      - **plus** – more

      - **moins** – less

      - **meilleur (m)** – better

      - **meilleure (f)** – better

    - Practice sentences:
      - **Ce film est plus intéressant.** (This movie is more interesting.)

      - **Elle est moins gentille que lui.** (She is less kind than him.)

      - **Ma série est meilleure.** (My series is better.)

    - **Checkpoint**: Write 5 sentences comparing things you like.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-07 08:00>


*** Lesson 6: Superlative Forms (Most)
    - Learn superlative forms using *le plus* (the most) and *le moins* (the least):

      - **le plus** – the most

      - **le moins** – the least

    - Practice sentences:

      - **C’est le film le plus intéressant.** (It’s the most interesting movie.)

      - **Elle est la plus gentille.** (She is the kindest.)

    - **Checkpoint**: Write 5 superlative sentences.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-09 08:00>


*** Lesson 7: Passé Composé (Past Tense)

    - Learn how to form the passé composé using auxiliary verbs:
      - **avoir** (to have): **J’ai mangé** (I ate)
      - **être** (to be): **Je suis allé** (I went)

    - Practice with regular verbs:
      - **manger (to eat)**: **J’ai mangé** (I ate)
      - **parler (to speak)**: **J’ai parlé** (I spoke)
    - **Checkpoint**: Write 5 sentences in the passé composé.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-11 08:00>


*** Lesson 8: Passé Composé Practice
    - Practice with irregular verbs in passé composé:
      - **avoir (to have)**: **J’ai eu** (I had)
      - **être (to be)**: **J’ai été** (I was)
      - **faire (to do)**: **J’ai fait** (I did)
    - Practice sentences:
      - **J’ai fait mes devoirs.** (I did my homework.)
      - **Il a eu un accident.** (He had an accident.)
    - **Checkpoint**: Write 5 sentences using passé composé with irregular verbs.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-13 08:00>

** Week 5-6: Speaking & Listening Practice + Future Tense
*** Lesson 9: Listening & Speaking
    - Watch a French TV show or listen to a podcast. Focus on pronunciation and understanding:
      - **Comment ça va ?** (How’s it going?)
      - **Ça va bien.** (I’m doing well.)
      - **Qu’est-ce que tu fais ?** (What are you doing?)
    - Practice repeating phrases you hear.
    - **Checkpoint**: Listen to 10 minutes of French and summarize.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-15 08:00>


*** Lesson 10: Future Tense (Future Proche)
    - Learn how to form the future tense using *aller + infinitive* (going to):
      - **Je vais manger** (I’m going to eat)
      - **Tu vas parler** (You’re going to speak)
    - Practice sentences:
      - **Je vais travailler demain.** (I’m going to work tomorrow.)
      - **Ils vont voyager en France.** (They are going to travel to France.)
    - **Checkpoint**: Write 5 sentences about future plans.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-17 08:00>


*** DONE Lesson 11: Speaking Practice
    - Practice speaking with a language partner or tutor. Focus on conversation starters and questions:
      - **Où habites-tu ?** (Where do you live?)
      - **Que fais-tu le week-end ?** (What do you do on weekends?)
    - **Checkpoint**: Have a 10-minute conversation in French.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-19 08:00>


*** Lesson 12: Future Tense Practice
    - Continue practicing future tense with regular and irregular verbs:
      - **Je vais étudier.** (I’m going to study.)
      - **Tu vas manger.** (You’re going to eat.)
    - **Checkpoint**: Write 10 sentences using future tense.
  
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-21 08:00>

** Week 7-8: Review & Advanced Sentence Construction
*** Lesson 13: Vocabulary Review & Advanced Sentences
    - Review all vocabulary learned so far.
    - Focus on constructing longer sentences and paragraphs.
    - **Checkpoint**: Write a short paragraph summarizing your day in French.
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-23 08:00>


*** Lesson 14: Conditional Tense Introduction
    - Learn the conditional tense (e.g., *je voudrais*, *j’aimerais*):
      - **je voudrais** – I would like

    - Practice sentences:
      - **Je voudrais un café.** (I would like a coffee.)
      - **J’aimerais visiter Paris.** (I would love to visit Paris.)
    - **Checkpoint**: Write 5 conditional sentences.
    SCHEDULED: <2025-02-25 08:00>


*** Lesson 15: Review & Fluency Practice
    - Review all tenses and vocabulary learned.
    - Focus on speaking fluently and without hesitation.
    - **Checkpoint**: Hold a 15-minute conversation in French, summarizing all topics learned.
